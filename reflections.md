Reflection 1 :

I think the Strategic/Network Planning Manager will disagree the most with the next action because their decisions affect the whole logistics network. Opening or closing a warehouse may improve one KPI, such as transportation distance, but it can also increase costs or create other operational problems. Therefore, they need to consider the overall impact before making a decision.


Which assumption would you least want to defend to a real client and why?
The assumption I would least want to defend is estimating demand from population (50 tonnes per year per 1,000 inhabitants). Population does not necessarily reflect a city's actual transport demand.

Choose two KPI constructs that point toward different actions for the same warehouse.
For Lyon, its high CO₂ emissions suggest optimizing the routes it serves. However, its high storage costs (€7,788,952/year) suggest improving inventory and storage capacity management. These KPIs highlight two different improvement priorities for the same warehouse.

Inspect actual top-10 CO2 routes; would you trust #1 for consolidation or double-check something?
The highest-emission route is Marseille ← Lyon. I would investigate it first, but I would not automatically consider it the best consolidation opportunity. I would double-check shipment volumes and delivery schedules to determine whether combining deliveries is actually feasible.

For highest-total-cost warehouse, which lever (transport/storage) first and which actual number convinced you?
I would focus on storage costs at Lyon first. They amount to €7,788,952/year, compared with €5,687,061/year for transport. This makes storage the larger cost component and the first lever I would investigate.

Recommend open Bordeaux/Marseille, close Lille, or baseline; how does capex/lease-exit/non-distance value caveat affect confidence; did Reflection 1 prediction hold?
I would recommend investigating the opening of a warehouse in Marseille, as it achieves the lowest average distance (115.03 km), compared with 168.04 km for the baseline network. However, investment costs, lease-exit costs and strategic factors beyond distance are not considered, so further analysis is necessary before making a final decision. The dashboards also illustrate why stakeholders may disagree: each prioritizes a different objective.
